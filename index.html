Who We Are

A diversified South African group built on trust and delivery.

Mbulaheni Group is a premier black-owned holding company headquartered in Polokwane, Limpopo. Since 2015, we have grown into a multi-sector group serving businesses, government entities, and households across the continent.

Our five divisions — civil engineering, managed IT, fuel supply, commercial cleaning, and private security — combine international standards with deep local expertise to deliver outcomes that last.

We are committed to transforming communities through job creation, skills development, and sustainable business practices that make a measurable difference in people's lives.
